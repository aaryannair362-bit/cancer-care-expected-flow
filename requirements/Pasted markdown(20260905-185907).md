## C.18.0 — Surgical Oncology module design principles `[PRODUCT]`

Surgery was the shallowest domain in the prior build. The following principles are stated once and govern every screen in this section; they are not optional design flourishes.

**SO-001 [PRODUCT]** — Surgical Oncology is built around a strict three-object dependency chain, structurally identical in spirit to the Radiotherapy Prescription → Plan → Delivery chain (`RT-002`) and the five-value dose chain (`GEN-DOS-001`):

`SURGICAL PLAN (intended)` → `OPERATIVE NOTE (actual)` → `HISTOPATHOLOGY REVIEW (result)` → `ADJUVANT HANDOFF (consequence)`

Each is a separately versioned, separately signed object. No object may be inferred from, merged with, or silently derived from another. **Planned procedure and actual procedure performed are never the same field** (`GEN-CMP-001`), at any point from consent through the completion summary.

**SO-002 [PRODUCT]** — **Site and laterality** are rendered with the highest visual prominence available in the design system (large type, fixed non-scrolling position, colour-differentiated from all other header content) on every surgical screen from first consultation through discharge, and must be **independently reconfirmed by explicit affirmative action** — never inherited by default — at each of five checkpoints: Surgical Plan signature, Consent, Pre-operative H&P, Theatre Safety Gate (both Sign-In and Time-Out), and Operative Note opening. A mismatch detected between any two checkpoints is a **hard stop** that blocks progression until reconciled by a named authorised role, with the discrepancy permanently retained in the audit trail.

**SO-003 [PRODUCT]** — The surgical pathway from plan to theatre is a **sequential gated workflow**, structurally identical to the Day Care gating model (`MAR-001`). A case cannot enter Theatre Readiness (`SCR-SO-008`) until each of the following is individually satisfied or explicitly overridden with a recorded reason and an authorising role: Surgical Plan signed · pre-operative workup complete · anaesthesia fitness recorded · blood availability confirmed where required · consent obtained and current · scheduling confirmed · site/laterality reconfirmed. The screen must always show the gate sequence and name the specific gate currently blocking.

**SO-004 [PRODUCT]** — Every specimen removed at operation is a first-class traceable object from the moment of excision, structurally identical in rigor to pharmacy's lot-level traceability (`PHA-100`): `SPECIMEN (labelled in theatre)` → `PATHOLOGY ACCESSION` → `FINAL HISTOPATHOLOGY` → `PATHOLOGICAL STAGE` → `SURGEON REVIEW` → `ADJUVANT HANDOFF`. Every specimen carries its own identifier from creation; specimen identity is never re-keyed by pathology from a paper label alone where barcode/2D-code capture is configured `[CCA CONFIG]`.

**SO-005 [PRODUCT]** — Clinical stage (cTNM) is never overwritten by pathological stage (pTNM). Both are permanently retained, independently dated and attributed, and displayed side by side wherever staging appears in a surgical context (`GEN-CMP-001`, `SCR-DX-002`).

**SO-006 [PRODUCT]** — Anaesthesia (`B.21.8`), Blood Bank/Transfusion (`B.21.9`) and Stoma/Wound Care Nursing (`B.21.7`) are separately role-owned clinical domains. Surgical Oncology screens display their outputs **read-only**, sourced from the owning role's own signed record, with source and timestamp shown. The surgical team can never edit an anaesthetist's fitness decision, a blood bank's crossmatch/issue record, or a stoma/wound nurse's independent assessment (Part A, role-ownership principle). Where this document specifies fields such as "ASA grade" or "crossmatch status" within a surgical screen, they are imports, not surgical-team entries.

**SO-007 [PRODUCT]** — Post-operative complication severity is recorded against a versioned, configurable grading scale `[CCA CONFIG]` (an institutionally adopted surgical complication classification, its grade definitions, and its escalation implications). The grading *engine* — value set structure, mandatory grading at defined checkpoints, linkage to discharge and adjuvant-timing logic, longitudinal display — is `[PRODUCT]`. No grading scale or grade-definition text is invented in this document.

**SO-008 [PRODUCT]** — Drains and stomas are longitudinal **per-device** objects, structurally identical to the vascular-access device register pattern established for Day Care and the equipment register pattern established for Radiotherapy (`RTT-008`, `SCR-MAR-009`'s device handling). Each physical drain or stoma has its own identifier, its own insertion event, a **time-series of observations** (never a single mutable "output" field), and its own removal/closure or ongoing-management event.

**SO-009 [PRODUCT]** — Every operative and post-operative document capable of affecting adjuvant-therapy timing (Operative Note, Post-operative Complication Note, Surgical Discharge) must expose a structured, machine-readable **"Affects adjuvant timing"** flag with a coded reason, consumed directly by the Adjuvant Handoff screen (`SCR-SO-018`). A clinically important delay must be discoverable from structured data, never only from free text buried in a note.

**SO-010 [PRODUCT]** — Every counted, weighed or measured intra-operative quantity that safety practice requires to reconcile (swabs, needles, instruments) is captured as a **structured count record with an explicit correct/incorrect outcome and a mandatory discrepancy-resolution workflow** before the operative note may be signed. A discrepancy is never resolved by silently editing the count.

**SO-011 [PRODUCT]** — Implants and surgically placed devices (mesh, prosthesis, expander, marker/fiducial, drain, port) are written to the same cross-cutting **device register** referenced elsewhere in this product (`RTT` device handling, `MAR` vascular-access device handling), carrying item, manufacturer, model, serial/lot, and implantation site, so that a recall or device query can traverse from device to patient regardless of which module implanted it.
