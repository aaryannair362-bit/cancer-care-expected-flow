OpenMRS Oncology historical regimen reference content
====================================================

Source repository: https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-module-oncology
Selected source files: regimens/AC.yaml, regimens/CHOP.yaml, regimens/CarboTaxol.yaml

V12 imports selected regimen structure/data as historical reference content only.
Imported rows are NON-ORDERABLE until an institution-reviewed working version supplies current
clinical and pharmacy safety rules, maps every medication to the local formulary, records
independent Medical Oncology and Oncology Pharmacy review, and is explicitly activated.

No imported historical regimen should be interpreted as current prescribing guidance.
